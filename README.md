### David Brown, PhD

I develop data synthesis methods and tools that turn heterogeneous data into information that guides agricultural and environmental decision-making. Postdoctoral Associate at Cornell University; previously 17 years at Bioversity International (CGIAR).

**Current work: [MERLIN](https://merlin-64652886693.us-east1.run.app/)**
A decision-support tool, commissioned by the Gates Foundation, that estimates the environmental and socioeconomic impacts of livestock interventions, including livestock and crop emissions, land use, water use, household income and nutrition. Built with R/Shiny and deployed on Google Cloud Run. [Code](https://MERLIN-REPO-URL)

**Selected open tools**
- [ag5Tools](https://cran.r-project.org/package=ag5Tools): R package for AgERA5 agrometeorological data, 16,000+ CRAN downloads
- [gosset](https://cran.r-project.org/package=gosset): R package for analysis and synthesis of ranking data from on-farm trials
